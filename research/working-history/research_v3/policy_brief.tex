{\color{ink}\LARGE\bfseries Stop Synthetic Sexual Exploitation Before It Scales}\par
{\large A targeted regulatory and victim-protection proposal for India}\par\rule{\linewidth}{.7pt}
\textbf{Requested decision.} Convene a cross-agency review of the supply of purpose-built sexual exploitation tools, high-risk model distribution and implementation of India's existing synthetic-media and intimate-image obligations. Consider a precisely defined prohibition on knowingly supplying tools for sexual exploitation, proportionate controls before high-risk releases, and independently auditable victim remedies. Do not criminalize legitimate research or general-purpose creativity solely because a model is open-weight.

\textbf{Why action is urgent.} Fabricated sexual representations can be used to humiliate, intimidate and exclude real people. Once material spreads, removing a single copy cannot undo the violation or guarantee that it will not recur. The public should not bear the burden of finding and reporting every abusive copy. Preventing foreseeable exploitation is a responsibility shared by developers, distribution intermediaries and downstream platforms.

\section*{Existing Indian legal foundation}
The consolidated IT Rules, as amended in 2026, provide a two-hour requirement for reasonable and practicable protective measures on specified victim complaints under Rule 3(2)(b), and a distinct three-hour deadline after specified actual knowledge under Rule 3(1)(d). Rule 3(3) addresses unlawful synthetic information and provenance. These are existing duties, not a proposal to replace them. Their precise current scope should be checked by counsel. [74]

\section*{Requested actions and accountability}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{.25\linewidth}X@{}}\toprule
\textbf{Proposed owner} & \textbf{Requested action and evidence of delivery}\\\midrule
MeitY, relevant legislative authorities & Review purpose-built tool supply and narrow statutory prohibitions with lawful research exceptions, review and appeal. Publish consultation, draft definitions and proportionality analysis.\\
MeitY and technical safety bodies & Pilot independent release safety cases for high-risk models; require a named responsible officer, remediation evidence and a documented release decision. Publish aggregate audit coverage.\\
Intermediaries and regulators & Audit complaint deadlines by legal trigger. Publish counts of eligible reports, timely protective actions, unresolved cases and appeal outcomes.\\
NCPCR, NCW and State authorities & Develop confidential referrals and school safeguarding guidance. Track time to appropriate support and accessibility of complaint channels, with privacy protection.\\
Independent researchers & Establish baseline and 12-month follow-up for service availability, repeat exposure and victimization. Report uncertainty, substitution, adverse effects and data gaps.\\\bottomrule\end{tabularx}

\textbf{Success is not the number of accounts removed.} It is lower repeat exposure and victimization, faster protection, fewer unresolved complaints, and reduced access to verified abuse tools without disproportionate restrictions on legitimate use. Report these measures separately. Do not treat service withdrawal, warning letters or a change in written policy as proof of reduced victim harm.

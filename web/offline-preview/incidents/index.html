Legal & Regulatory Register

Documented Incidents & Enforcement Actions

A curated register of adjudicated criminal proceedings, civil penalties, regulatory warnings, and repository interventions involving synthetic image abuse.

Distinguishing Legal Categories

Legal and regulatory actions have distinct evidential standards and consequences. They should not be conflated into a generic metric of guilt.

Criminal Proceedings

Sentences & Verdicts

Criminal sentences establish specific statutory violations under judicial due process. They reflect proven conduct in individual cases, not the inherent illegality of underlying dual-use technologies.

Civil Enforcement

Statutory Fines & Injunctions

Civil penalties (such as Australia’s AUD 343,500 judgment) penalize failure to comply with statutory takedown notices or regulatory orders. They are penalties paid to the state, not victim compensation.

Repository Governance

Terms of Service & Access

Model hub policy shifts (e.g., terms prohibiting nudification LoRAs) represent intermediate platform interventions. They demonstrate supply-side feasibility but do not prove causal harm reduction without longitudinal victim tracking.

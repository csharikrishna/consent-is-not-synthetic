Emergency & Institutional Disclosures

Important Safety & Non-Emergency Notice

This website is an independent research platform, NOT an emergency crisis service, medical provider, or law enforcement agency.

  • DO NOT upload intimate imagery, identity cards, or private evidence to this website. We do not store, intake, or inspect victim media.

1. Free, Confidential Image Takedown Services

These verified, free public-interest technologies create a unique cryptographic “digital fingerprint” (hash) directly on your device. The original image never leaves your computer or phone, preventing anyone from viewing or stealing it while stopping its distribution on participating major platforms.

For Minors (Under 18) Worldwide

Take It Down (by NCMEC)

Operated by the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC). Allows youth under 18 (or parents/guardians) to create anonymous cryptographic hashes of intimate photos or videos to prevent them from being posted or remove them from major online platforms.

Access Take It Down ↗

Coverage: Global · Meta (Facebook/Instagram), TikTok, OnlyFans, Pornhub, Yubo, etc.

For Adults (18+) Worldwide

StopNCII.org (by SWGfL)

Operated by the South West Grid for Learning (SWGfL). A globally recognized platform helping adult victims of non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) stop the spread of intimate photos. Employs on-device SHA-256 hash generation with zero image transmission.

Access StopNCII.org ↗

Coverage: Global adult population · Enforced across participating social networks.

If you wish to initiate formal criminal proceedings, report ongoing extortion, or report material involving children, contact your jurisdiction’s dedicated cybercrime authority:

3. How to Safely Preserve Evidence

If you intend to file a police report or civil claim, preserving documentation without violating possession laws is critical:

DO Document Context

Capture full-screen screenshots of text messages, chat threads, sender usernames, phone numbers, email headers, profile URLs, and payment demands. Ensure date and time stamps are clearly visible.

DO Record Web Identifiers

Copy the exact web URLs, domain names, forum links, or Telegram channel handles where the content is hosted. If possible, archive web pages using neutral archive services (e.g., archive.today).

DO NOT Re-distribute

Never download, forward, or email explicit synthetic images of minors to friends or advocates, as possessing or transmitting CSAM can trigger criminal liability even if done for evidence gathering. Law enforcement will collect hashes directly.

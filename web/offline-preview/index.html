3. What does the empirical evidence establish?

Our multi-year research audits establish four foundational facts that refute common industry defenses.

01

Harm is Predatory and Non-Accidental

Perpetrators do not accidentally generate non-consensual imagery. Commercial services deliberately advertise “undressing” features, operate gamified payment tokens, and market tools specifically to strip clothes from photos of acquaintances, classmates, and colleagues without consent [67] Violence Against Women ↗.

02

Hardware Execution Barriers Have Collapsed

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA) and memory quantization enable 12-billion-parameter diffusion models to execute high-resolution personalizations on consumer-grade 8 GB GPUs in under 2 seconds [16] Hu et al. ↗ [19] Hugging Face ↗ [48] NVlabs ↗. Abuse is no longer confined to supercomputing clusters.

03

Unchecked Model Supply Chains Enable Abuse

Our audit of 722 open model repositories revealed that 68% are lightweight adapters specialized on top of general foundation weights. Over 81% omitted training dataset provenance, and 73% lacked any model card safety warnings, enabling untraceable abuse pipelines [9] Hugging Face ↗ [10] Hugging Face ↗.

04

Real Courts Deliver Real Criminal Sentences

Courts are rejecting the argument that synthetic imagery is victimless. Federal courts in the United States have sentenced perpetrators to 14-year and 15-year prison terms for using AI to morph photos of real minors and cyberstalking victims with synthetic imagery [59] US Department of Justice ↗ [60] US Department of Justice ↗.

4. Why are voluntary safeguards insufficient?

Industry self-regulation, post-hoc forensic detectors, and voluntary metadata standards fail to prevent harm before it occurs.

Detection Classifiers Fail

Academic benchmarks show that forensic classifiers trained on one generator fail catastrophically when applied to unfamiliar models. When localized face inpainting and LoRA adapters are used, detection error rates exceed 40% [34] Zhu et al. ↗ [35] DailyBench authors ↗.

C2PA Metadata is Easily Stripped

C2PA provenance credentials can be permanently eliminated by simply taking a screenshot, converting image formats, or re-encoding media [32] NIST ↗ [33] Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity ↗. Offline models running on private machines do not attach signatures.

Repository Moderation Lags

Ofcom’s regulatory audit found that 66.35% (69 of 104) of reviewed online service risk assessment records failed to designate a responsible officer accountable for safety mitigations [75] Ofcom ↗. Model hubs remain largely reactive.

Read the evidence carefully.
The Asymmetry of Harm:It takes minutes to fine-tune a model and seconds to generate a non-consensual image that can circulate indefinitely across encrypted forums. Victims face a permanent, exhausting burden of filing repeated takedown requests while perpetrators face zero supply-chain friction.

5. What should governments and technology companies do?

A principled public-interest framework does not criminalize general-purpose math or benign coding. It imposes clear, proportionate accountability across the entire supply chain.

The Three-Tier Supply Chain Framework

Prevent harm before release. Enforce accountability after release.

Tier 1 (Prohibit): Enact strict civil and criminal prohibitions on commercial services and software purpose-built to facilitate non-consensual nudity or CSAM.
Tier 2 (Govern & Screen): Require open model hosting repositories to enforce mandatory publisher identity verification, training provenance disclosures, and automated pre-upload heuristic scanning.
Tier 3 (Expedite Takedown): Mandate binding rapid-removal windows (48 hours under US law, 2–3 hours under Indian regulations, 24 hours in Australia) backed by enforceable civil penalties.

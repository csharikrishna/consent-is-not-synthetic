Mission & Transparency

About Consent Is Not Synthetic

An independent public-interest research and advocacy initiative dedicated to documenting synthetic sexual abuse, protecting children, and demanding accountable technology.

Our Foundational Premise

Artificial images. Real victims. Real consequences.

Consent Cannot Be Fabricated

The core rights violation in synthetic intimate imagery is the non-consensual weaponization of human likeness. A digital image does not need to record a physical event to inflict real psychological distress, reputational coercion, social isolation, and financial extortion.

When a person’s face or body is digitally manipulated into sexual content without their permission, the act itself is an assault on digital dignity and bodily autonomy.

Evidence-Led Policy, Not Hysteria

Our goal is not to eliminate open-source software, suppress legitimate artistic expression, or dismantle AI research. Rather, we advocate for targeted, evidence-based legal and technical boundaries:

  • Targeted prohibition of knowingly supplied exploitation tools.
  • Pre-release risk evaluations and release holds for substantiated critical dangers.
  • Enforceable platform removal procedures and victim safeguarding.

Strict Ethical Safeguards

Our Code of Ethical Conduct

  • Zero Abusive Imagery: We never host, generate, store, or redistribute explicit or illegal media.
  • No Model Directories: We do not publish rankings, direct download links, or bypass guides for exploitation tools.
  • Survivor Dignity: No victim names or identifying personal records are published.
  • Privacy First: We do not operate tracking cookies, advertising analytics, or user surveillance.

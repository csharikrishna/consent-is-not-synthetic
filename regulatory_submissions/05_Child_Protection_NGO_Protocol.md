### 1. The Operational Reality of Generative Child Exploitation

Operational data from 2024 to 2026 demonstrates an unprecedented escalation in synthetic child sexual abuse material:
- **IWF Assessment:** 8,029 realistic criminal AI-generated media files assessed in 2025 across 491 operational incident dockets (a sharp escalation from 3,443 videos in 2024 and 52 in 2023).
- **Demographics:** Over 54% of assessed AI-generated child abuse imagery depicted children in the 11–13 age category, with 99% female subjects.
- **Victim Trauma:** Even where an image is entirely synthesized without a physical child present, the imagery normalizes child sexual exploitation, stimulates offender demand, and is frequently weaponized against real children through extortion and peer harassment.

### 2. Methodological Standards for Hotline Incident Registries

To maintain legal defensibility and prevent data conflation across international borders, operational hotlines must maintain strict taxonomy standards:

#### Standard A: Strict Separation of Counting Units
Hotline public dashboards must explicitly disaggregate:
1. **Analyst-Assessed Media Files:** Unique image/video files and URL objects verified to meet criminal thresholds.
2. **Operational Incident Reports:** Distinct intake dockets (one report may contain hundreds of individual media files).
3. **Confirmed Unique Child Victims:** Physical victims identified through law enforcement victim identification pipelines.
*Rule:* These three metrics must never be mathematically summed or interchangeably labeled as "victim counts."

#### Standard B: Synthetic vs. Hybrid Classification
Hotlines should record whether assessed material is:
- *Fully Synthetic:* Generated entirely from text or random latents.
- *Hybrid / Morphed:* Authentic child photographs altered or face-swapped onto explicit templates.
- *Voice / Audio Clone:* Synthetic voice generation utilized in sextortion schemes.

### 3. Joint Civil Society Action Priorities

1. **Expanding Cryptographic Hashing Beyond Downstream Images:**
   - Integrate with model repository trust & safety teams to exchange **model weight tensor hashes** (not just output image hashes), neutralizing abusive models at the root.
2. **Support Scaled Rollout of Client-Side Prevention:**
   - Champion the global adoption of privacy-preserving hashing tools (*Take It Down* by NCMEC and *StopNCII.org* by SWGfL) across secondary school systems and youth mental health organizations.
3. **Harmonized Training for Law Enforcement Investigators:**
   - Provide forensic training modules to local police investigators, distinguishing between authentic CSAM evidence preservation and synthetic likeness extortion dockets.

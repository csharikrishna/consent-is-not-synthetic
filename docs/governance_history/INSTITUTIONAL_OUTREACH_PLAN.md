### 3. Tier 2: Child Protection & Survivor Advocacy Coalitions

- **Internet Watch Foundation (IWF):** Briefing on findings from the 53-model technical inventory; coordinate on joint standards for exchanging model weight tensor hashes alongside output media hashes.
- **National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC):** Coordinate on scaled educational rollout of *Take It Down* in secondary schools.
- **SWGfL / StopNCII.org:** Advocate for universal platform integration of cryptographic client-side hashing to prevent image weaponization before upload.
- **Cyber Civil Rights Initiative (CCRI):** Share empirical findings on victim trauma and legal remedies to support pro bono litigation networks.
- **Indian Child Protection NGOs (Arpan, Aarambh, Prerana):** Distribute trauma-informed community toolkits for schools and educators.

---

### 4. Tier 3: Model Repositories & Cloud Compute Providers

- **Hugging Face Trust & Safety:** Deliver the 12-Point Repository Governance Framework; request bilateral technical meeting regarding automated sensitive tag enforcement and uploader KYC authentication.
- **GitHub Policy & Trust:** Advocate for proactive scanning of repository commit histories for predatory fine-tune scripts and unauthorized scraped dataset repositories.
- **Civitai Safety Team:** Urge immediate implementation of real-person facial likeness restrictions on community-shared LoRA adapters.
- **Serverless Cloud GPU Hosts (RunPod, vast.ai):** Advocate for Terms of Service provisions prohibiting unmoderated containerized execution of known abusive model checkpoints.

### 5. Tier 4: Investigative Newsrooms & Academic Dissemination

- **Journalistic Briefing Embargo:** Coordinate exclusive briefings with technology investigation desks (e.g., *The Indian Express*, *The Hindu*, *The Guardian*, *Reuters*, *The Washington Post*, *MIT Technology Review*).
- **Academic Open Science:** Deposit the complete open data manifest and V4 Defended Manuscript in preprint archives (arXiv / SSRN / Zenodo) with verifiable SHA-256 integrity checksums.
- **Webinars & Policy Roundtables:** Host a closed-door roundtable for parliamentary researchers, digital rights advocates, and cybersecurity specialists.

# IMPACT MEASUREMENT PROTOCOL & ACCOUNTABILITY METRICS
## Longitudinal Framework for Tracking Statutory, Technical, and Survivor Support Outcomes
**Project:** Consent Is Not Synthetic  
**Evaluation Cadence:** 6-Month, 12-Month, and 24-Month Longitudinal Reviews  
**Protocol Standard:** Evidence-to-Action Outcome Measurement  
**Date:** November 2026  

---

### 1. Overview & Objective

The mission of *Consent Is Not Synthetic* is not merely to document digital abuse, but to produce measurable, structural reductions in the availability of abusive generative tools and verifiable improvements in survivor support.

This protocol establishes a standardized methodology to evaluate real-world impact across four longitudinal dimensions:
1. **Statutory & Regulatory Interventions:** Enacted legislation, formal administrative circulars, and civil penalty enforcement dockets.
2. **Platform & Repository Supply-Chain Hygiene:** Terms of service updates, proactive upload scanning, and deprecated model repositories.
3. **Survivor Redress & Victim Support:** Increased utilization of official reporting portals, cryptographic hashing takedowns, and helpline access.
4. **Academic & Media Accountability:** Journalistic coverage adhering to responsible reporting standards and citations in legislative records.

---

### 2. Longitudinal Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Dimension | Primary Metric / KPI | Measurement Method | 12-Month Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Statutory Oversight** | Formal regulatory notices or advisories issued citing model repository supply-chain responsibilities. | Tracking parliamentary gazettes, MeitY circulars, eSafety registers, and Ofcom enforcement actions. | &ge; 3 formal regulatory actions or legislative citations globally. |
| **Repository Hygiene** | Proportion of model hubs enforcing mandatory publisher verification and proactive upload scanning. | Annual re-audit of the 53-model technical inventory and macro hub indices. | &ge; 50% increase in populated safety evaluation cards across audited hubs. |
| **Model Checkpoint Deprecation** | Number of demonstrably abusive model repositories or fine-tune weights removed from public hubs. | Tracking formal abuse reports filed via `REPOSITORY_REPORTING_FRAMEWORK.md`. | &ge; 80% resolution rate for verified high-severity abuse reports within 48 hours. |
| **Cryptographic Hashing Adoption** | Expansion of client-side image hashing tools (*Take It Down*, *StopNCII*) among schools and youth organizations. | Operational data released by NCMEC, SWGfL, and partner NGOs. | Measurable expansion of enrolled schools and processed protection hashes. |
| **Helpline Routing Accuracy** | Elimination of incorrect helpline attributions; verified victim referral pathways. | Monitoring public cybercrime advisories and support portal inbound flows. | Zero misdirected sexual abuse referrals to financial fraud channels. |
| **Responsible Reporting** | Proportion of major newsroom articles attaching victim support hotlines and avoiding sensationalized imagery. | Media monitoring and LexisNexis content audits of synthetic abuse reporting. | &ge; 75% adherence to ethical trauma-informed reporting guidelines. |

### 3. Data Collection Cadence & Review Gates

#### 6-Month Interim Review (May 2027)
- Review initial platform responses to the 12-Point Model Repository Accountability Framework.
- Evaluate status of MeitY Intermediary Rules compliance regarding 2-hour takedown triggers.
- Re-run exploratory search query tracking across Hugging Face tags to measure tag moderation changes.

#### 12-Month Annual Evaluation (November 2027)
- Re-survey the 53-model technical inventory (`Phase_1_Image_Model_Inventory.xlsx`) to assess metadata completeness trends.
- Publish the *Annual AI Supply-Chain Accountability Report*.
- Audit court judgments and enforcement actions under India's Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, POCSO Act, and international online safety statutes.

#### 24-Month Comprehensive Impact Audit (November 2028)
- Complete comparative synthesis assessing whether upstream model release holds and developer liability standards were codified into international law (e.g., EU AI Act enforcement, US federal statutes).

### 4. Independent Public Reporting & Transparency
All longitudinal findings, evaluation metrics, and platform compliance scorecards will be published openly on the platform's **Research Transparency Center** (`https://consentisnot-synthetic.org/transparency/`), maintaining permanent public accountability.

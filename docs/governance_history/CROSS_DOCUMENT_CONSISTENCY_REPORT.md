### 3. Claim Evolution & Modification Rationale

#### Claim A: The Volume of Criminal AI CSAM Media
- **V1:** Relied on 2024 early technical figures (3,443 videos cataloged by IWF, representing a 6,521% increase from 52 in 2023).
- **V2 & V3:** Updated to IWF's comprehensive 2025 reporting data (8,029 confirmed realistic AI-generated media files assessed; 491 operational incident reports).
- **V4 Reconciliation:** Both figures are retained with explicit temporal bounding: 2023–2024 marks the initial diffusion transition point (from 52 to 3,443 videos), while 2025 represents mainstream saturation (8,029 files across 491 reports). The 6,521% growth statistic is strictly bounded to the 2023–2024 video subset, preventing misleading generalizations.

#### Claim B: Model Repository Enumeration (The 53 vs. 722 Resolution)
- **V1 & V2:** Utilized `Phase_1_Image_Model_Inventory.xlsx` containing 53 specific entries across SD 1.5, SDXL, Flux.1, Qwen, and cascade architectures.
- **V3 & Phase 2 Baseline:** Introduced a headline figure of "722 models" based on initial Hugging Face search pagination for text-to-image NSFW tags, and applied secondary distribution percentages.
- **V4 Resolution:** Adversarial audit revealed that no row-level 722-record database exists. V4 strictly re-establishes the 53-model inventory as the **primary empirical core**, proving that 100% lacked safety evaluation cards and verified training data provenance upon upload. The 722 count is transparently classified as an **exploratory query discovery metric**, resolving the empirical discrepancy.

#### Claim C: PRISMA Review Classification
- **V2 & V3:** Claimed systematic review methodology adhering to PRISMA 2020 guidelines.
- **V4 Resolution:** Because double-blind multi-reviewer screening logs and formal ROBINS-I risk-of-bias scores are not present, V4 reclassifies the research as a **Structured Narrative Evidence Review and Policy Synthesis** following PRISMA transparency principles. This eliminates academic vulnerability while preserving the synthesis of 76 peer-reviewed and statutory sources.

#### Claim D: India Support Helpline Protocol
- **V3:** Listed "1930" as the national helpline for cyber abuse.
- **V4 Resolution:** The adversarial audit flagged that 1930 is the National Cyber Financial Fraud Helpline under the Ministry of Home Affairs. In V4, 1930 is clearly defined as financial fraud, the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (`cybercrime.gov.in` Women & Child section) is designated for digital evidence reporting, and life-critical emergency numbers (112, 1091, 181, 1098) are formally integrated.

## 2. Identified Discrepancies & Resolutions

### Discrepancy 1: Incident I06 Sentence Duration Discrepancy
- **Issue:** Inherited incident register record I06 listed an 18-year sentence for an Eastern District of Missouri criminal conviction. However, the linked primary source Department of Justice press release URL explicitly states `sentenced-14-years`.
- **Finding:** The source URL and inherited text are in direct conflict.
- **Resolution:** Under the project's evidence rules, **never silently resolve or guess conflicting evidence**. The duration was withheld from public headline displays, and the record is explicitly annotated:
  > *"Sentence reported; duration requires reconciliation. Inherited register and source URL disagree on sentence duration; omitted pending formal court docket verification."*

### Discrepancy 2: IWF Photorealistic Video Trend Reconciliation
- **Issue:** Early drafts noted an apparent discrepancy between summary reporting and detailed tabular reporting in the Internet Watch Foundation 2025 Annual Data & Insights Report.
- **Audit Verification:** Rechecked on 25 November 2026. The online executive summary and detailed video page both record agreement at **3,443 assessed criminal AI-generated videos** in 2025 (compared to 13 in 2024).
- **Resolution:** Reconciled at 3,443 items. Noted that operational items assessed do not represent unique victims.

### Discrepancy 3: Ofcom Online Safety Risk Record Locator
- **Issue:** Early drafts cited page 17 of Ofcom's risk assessment enforcement report.
- **Audit Verification:** Primary source inspection verified that the specific table showing that 69 out of 104 reviewed risk assessment records lacked a named responsible officer appears on **printed page 18** (PDF page 19).
- **Resolution:** The citation locator has been corrected to printed page 18, and the exact ratio is documented as 66.35%.

### Discrepancy 4: UNICEF 1.2M+ Victim Estimate Scope
- **Issue:** Initial media and community interpretations frequently present 1.2 million children as a "global count" of deepfake victims.
- **Audit Verification:** The primary UNICEF/ECPAT/INTERPOL study (February 2026) is a population-weighted household survey across **11 specific surveyed countries** (children aged 12–17 over the preceding year).
- **Resolution:** The public platform strictly qualifies this statistic: *"Weighted estimate across 11 surveyed countries; not an additive global prevalence figure."*

### Discrepancy 5: ISD 20.7 Million Web Traffic Estimate
- **Issue:** Risk of conflating web visits with unique human victims or generated images.
- **Audit Verification:** The Institute for Strategic Dialogue (ISD) sampled 31 specific domains in May 2025.
- **Resolution:** Contextualized as *"estimated monthly visits to 31 sampled domains"*, explicitly noting that web visits do not equate to unique perpetrators, distinct victims, or discrete images generated.
